Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TISSIR CONSULTING handles the personal data you submit through this website.

Data we collect

  • Your name, company, email address and phone/WhatsApp number, when you provide them.
  • The service you are interested in and the description of your project or request.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about your project.
  • To prepare a relevant proposal or follow-up, when appropriate.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Form submissions are delivered to our email inbox via Web3Forms, a third-party form service. We share data only with team members who need it to respond to your inquiry.

Retention

We keep inquiry data only as long as necessary to respond and for legitimate follow-up, then delete it on request.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing contact@tissirconsulting.com.
